abstract: >-
  This paper offers a unified treatment of two families of revocation mechanisms for the European Digital Identity Wallet: positive dynamic accumulators and signed-pairs. It shows that signed-pairs can be expressed in standard accumulator syntax, then compares four constructions both by their protocol properties and by their performance across the credential lifecycle on server and smartphone hardware, making explicit the trade-offs since no construction dominates in every aspect.
